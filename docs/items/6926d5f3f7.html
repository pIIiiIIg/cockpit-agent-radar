JoyAI-Talker: Full-Duplex Speech Interactive Large Model Built for Empathetic Voice Agents

论文Paper · arxiv · 2026-08-02 · ◈ 24
摘要Summary
JoyAI-Talker 由外置 160ms 语义门控、MoE Thinker 和可控 Talker 组成，在用户打断时响应率为 0.88，并能按说话人属性与情绪控制内容和副语言表达。其多状态判停可启发 StreamingModelHarness，但当前 Harness 仍是判停后生成、晚注入工具和外挂 TTS，不能称为原生模型级全双工。 We present JoyAI-Talker, a full-duplex speech dialogue system that delivers robust foundation model capabilities while empowering empathetic interaction and voice agent intelligence. JoyAI-Talker adopts a modular Thinker-Talker architecture and further implements a unified speech-text joint training pipeline to mitigate the common "cognitive degradation" bottleneck, thereby largely preserving the model's core textual reasoning, STEM, and logical capabilities while extending them to speech-based interaction. For expressive speech synthesis, the Talker module employs a text-controllable generation paradigm that enables natural-language instructions to flexibly control vocal attributes and localized paralinguistic events, such as laughter and sighs, supporting more expressive and fine-grained speech responses. To enhance conversational empathy, we introduce the Persona-Adaptive Empathetic Response (PAER) framework. PAER employs a hierarchical cognitive pipeline to extract non-verbal speaker cues, such as gender, age, and emotional state, from raw input audio, incorporate them into the Thinker's CoT reasoning, and generate context-adaptive responses that align semantically appropriate text with fine-grained control over utterance-level expressiveness and localized paralinguistic events, including sighs, speaking rate, and volume. We further integrate Joy-Duplex, a state-driven, plug-and-play full-duplex framework that functions as an efficient gating engine for real-time turn control. Extensive evaluations show that JoyAI-Talker achieves highly competitive performance on foundational T2T and S2T benchmarks. In full-duplex evaluation, the system reaches a high response rate of 0.88 under user interruptions while maintaining an extremely low false-trigger rate under background speech, demonstrating its readiness for fluid and natural speech dialogue.

深度讲解Deep dive

JoyAI-Talker 用外置 Joy-Duplex 语义门控、MoE Thinker 和可控 Talker 组成语音代理，在保留文本推理的同时处理打断、共情和副语言表达；它不是单模型内部的双音频流架构。

它解决什么问题Problem

语音联合训练常损伤原有推理与指令能力，简单 VAD 又难区分真实打断、附和和背景人声；系统还需要把年龄、情绪等非语言线索转成合适的内容与表达。

核心方法Core method

48.9B 总参数、约 3.28B 激活参数的 JoyAI-LLM Flash MoE 充当 Thinker，语音编码器输出 12.5Hz 表示。PAER 将说话人属性和情绪写入推理与表达控制；Talker 以自回归 Transformer 加因果 Diffusion Transformer 流式合成。独立的 1.7B Joy-Duplex 每 160ms 预测 partial、complete、accept 等状态，只有 accept 才触发后续回答。

怎么工作How it works

  1. 流式编码用户音频，每 160ms 更新转写和语义状态。
  2. Joy-Duplex 判断继续等待、附和、接受查询或拒绝背景语音。
  3. 收到 accept 后，Thinker 执行语义推理、工具决策和响应规划。
  4. PAER 产生正文、表达指令和局部叹息/笑声标记，再由 Talker 流式合成。

对 StreamingModelHarness 的帮助（编辑判断）Fit for StreamingModelHarness (editorial analysis)
  • 编辑判断：Joy-Duplex 的多状态语义门可映射到 StreamingModelHarness 的判停与背景语音拒绝层，accept 后再晚注入工具并生成。
  • 编辑判断：当前 Harness 是流式输入、判停后生成和外挂 TTS，不应据此宣称原生模型级全双工；论文也未证明其工具列表支持晚注入。

边界与局限Limits

  • 论文称系统缺乏对警报、音乐等一般环境声音的理解，复杂多约束指令与代理调用仍受限。
  • 模块边界会带来额外时延、声学细节损失和异步服务复杂度，正文没有把这些成本完整量化。
  • 截至 2026-08-04 未找到 JoyAI-Talker 专属代码、权重、训练数据或许可证。
