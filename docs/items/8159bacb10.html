BridgeVLA++: A Data-Efficient, Generalizable, and Memory-Augmented Vision-Language-Action Framework for 3D Manipulation

论文Paper · hf · 2026-08-04 · ◈ 6
摘要Summary
BridgeVLA++ 在 BridgeVLA 上加入统一时空记忆，显著提升记忆依赖双臂操作，真实场景记忆任务成功率由20.0%提升到93.3%。对 Harness 更可借鉴的是历史关键帧记忆与遮挡鲁棒定位，而非直接接入语音判停流水线。 Leveraging pre-trained vision-language models (VLMs) to construct vision-language-action (VLA) models has emerged as a promising paradigm for 3D robot manipulation. However, existing 3D VLA methods remain data-hungry, exhibit limited generalization under distribution shifts, and lack explicit memory of past observations. These limitations hinder their application to data-scarce, open-world, and memory-dependent manipulation scenarios. Our previous work, BridgeVLA, improves data efficiency and generalization by preserving the input--output alignment of a pre-trained VLM during 3D action learning: raw point clouds are projected into multi-view images, and intermediate heatmaps are predicted before generating robot actions. In this work, we develop BridgeVLA++ by equipping BridgeVLA with a unified spatio-temporal memory architecture that models persistent spatial context and temporal interaction history. The resulting memory-augmented framework can reason over observation histories while preserving BridgeVLA's data efficiency and generalization capabilities. Extensive experiments show that our framework achieves strong performance on spatial manipulation tasks while exhibiting robust generalization. BridgeVLA++ further achieves state-of-the-art performance on two challenging memory-dependent manipulation benchmarks without sacrificing the data efficiency and generalization of the original BridgeVLA. In addition, BridgeVLA++ performs effectively in bimanual manipulation settings and is validated on an additional real-world robotic platform, demonstrating its scalability across tasks, environments, and robotic platforms. These results establish BridgeVLA++ as a unified 3D vision-language-action framework that simultaneously supports data-efficient learning, robust generalization, and effective memory-aware robot manipulation. Project website: https://bridgevla-plus.github.io/.

深度讲解Deep dive

BridgeVLA++ 为3D VLA 增加统一时空记忆，在保持原 BridgeVLA 数据效率与泛化的同时，把真实记忆依赖任务平均成功率从20.0%提升到93.3%。

它解决什么问题Problem

语言条件操作常需跨时刻记忆与遮挡下的空间坚持；既有3D VLA 在记忆依赖任务上弱，而强行堆历史又易损害效率与泛化。

核心方法Core method

在 BridgeVLA 的多视图2D热图对齐框架上，加入时间记忆（锚点视图、历史关键帧、自适应子目标关键帧）与空间记忆（遮挡鲁棒细定位），并扩展双臂设置。

怎么工作How it works

  1. 点云投影为多视图图像，沿用2D热图预训练与3D动作微调。
  2. 时间记忆保存锚点与历史关键帧 token 以支持粗阶段推理。
  3. 空间记忆维持可重渲染的空间上下文以细化定位。
  4. 在 RLBench/COLOSSEUM/GemBench/RMBench/MemoryBench 与真机上评估。

论文报告了什么Reported findings

  • 原 BridgeVLA 在真机通用操作上平均比强基线高32%，并保持扰动/未见物体与指令鲁棒。
  • BridgeVLA++ 在记忆依赖真机任务上把平均成功率从20.0%提升到93.3%。
  • 在 RMBench/MemoryBench 等记忆基准上达到正文所称 SOTA，同时不牺牲原基准表现。
  • 消融显示去掉空间或时间记忆会损害遮挡高精度与记忆任务。
对 StreamingModelHarness 的帮助（编辑判断）Fit for StreamingModelHarness (editorial analysis)
  • 时空记忆分层（历史关键帧 + 空间缓存）可类比 Harness 对跨轮状态与晚到工具结果的外挂记忆。
  • 主战场是机器人3D操作，不是语音流式判停；不要直接当作座舱对话模型。
  • 若座舱接具身执行，可借鉴其记忆依赖评测拆分，而不是假设 VLA 能替代双工语音栈。

边界与局限Limits

  • 依赖多视图投影与热图监督，系统复杂度高于纯语音 harness。
  • 真机结果绑定 Franka/Dobot 等平台与任务集。
  • 开源以 BridgeVLA 家族仓库/项目页为主，需按任务确认 ++ 权重是否齐全。
