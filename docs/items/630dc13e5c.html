Negotiating Privacy with Smart Voice Assistants: Risk-Benefit and Control-Acceptance Tensions

论文Paper · arxiv · 2026-04-04 · ◈ 8
摘要Summary
基于469名加拿大青年调查，作者提出风险-收益与控制-接受两组隐私张力指数，并发现它们与隐私保护行为显著相关，高频语音助手用户更偏向收益/接受侧。对座舱始终在听的麦克风策略有产品与治理参考价值。 Smart Voice assistants (SVAs) are widely adopted by youth, yet privacy decision-making in these environments is often characterized by competing considerations rather than clear-cut preferences. While our prior research has examined privacy risks, benefits, trust, and self-efficacy as distinct predictors of behavior, less attention has been paid to how these factors combine into higher-level tension that shapes privacy outcomes. This study introduces a negotiation-based framework for understanding youth privacy decision-making with SVAs by operationalizing two composite indices: the Risk-Benefit Tension Index (RBTI) and the Control-Acceptance Tension Index (CATI), using survey data from 469 Canadian youth aged 16-24. We examine the distribution of these indices and their relationship with privacy-protective behavior and SVA usage. Results show that both indices are meaningfully associated with protective action. Frequent SVA usage exhibits more benefit-dominant and acceptance-leaning negotiation profiles, suggesting that convenience-driven engagement may come at the expense of perceived control. By reframing privacy decision-making as a process of negotiation rather than inconsistency, this study offers a complementary perspective on the privacy paradox and provides a compact measurement approach for capturing how youth navigate competing privacy pressures in voice-enabled ecosystems.

深度讲解Deep dive

基于469名加拿大青年调查，RBTI/CATI 两套隐私张力指数与隐私保护行为显著相关，高频语音助手用户更偏向收益与接受一侧。

它解决什么问题Problem

青年使用智能语音助手时的隐私决策常是风险与收益、控制与接受之间的拉扯，既有研究多把各因素拆开建模，难刻画这种谈判式权衡。

核心方法Core method

用 N=469 的加拿大16–24岁用户调查，定义 RBTI=z(风险)-z(收益)、CATI=控制相关量-z(收益)，分析分布，并以回归预测隐私保护行为（PPB），同时比较不同使用频率组别。

怎么工作How it works

  1. 收集感知风险、收益、控制/效能与隐私保护行为等量表。
  2. 标准化后合成 RBTI 与 CATI。
  3. 描述分布并做与 PPB、使用频率的相关/回归。
  4. 比较稀用与日用用户的张力剖面。

论文报告了什么Reported findings

  • RBTI 与 PPB 相关 r=.26（p<0.001），CATI 与 PPB 相关 r=.29（p<0.001）。
  • 回归中 CATI β=0.209（p<0.001），RBTI β=0.074（p=0.006），模型 R²=0.133。
  • 使用频率显著影响指数：F_RBTI(3,463)=44.76，F_CATI(3,463)=36.93，均 p<0.001；日用者 RBTI 更低（更偏收益）。
  • 样本中日用26.9%、周用24.1%、少用40.5%。
对 StreamingModelHarness 的帮助（编辑判断）Fit for StreamingModelHarness (editorial analysis)
  • 对座舱始终可听麦克风与云端日志策略，可用张力指数思维设计透明控制与默许风险提示。
  • 高频用户更偏接受便利，说明仅靠风险提示不够，需要可感知的控制能力。
  • 这是社科问卷研究，不提供可运行的 Harness 组件。

边界与局限Limits

  • 横断调查，不能推断因果。
  • 样本限加拿大青年，文化与车载场景外推有限。
  • 指数是线性合成，可能简化真实谈判过程。
