OSReward: Instituting Standardized Evaluation for Cross-Platform Computer-Use Reward Models

论文Paper · hf · 2026-07-29 · ◈ 3
摘要Summary
OSReward 用跨平台人工金标揭示 CUA judge 普遍会把未完成轨迹误判为成功，并训练了更低成本的 OS-Shepherd。它适合在 StreamingModelHarness 一轮 post-commit 工具轨迹结束后做结果验证或奖励建模，但不负责实时编排，当前训练流水线和数据许可证仍不完整。 Computer-using agents (CUAs) are advancing rapidly across the digital world. A CUA trajectory records the agent's actions, states, and reasoning. Verifying whether it fulfilled the task instruction is central to CUA evaluation, data curation, and reinforcement learning. Neither human-written verifiers nor human annotators can provide such verification at scale, so the field increasingly turns to vision-language models (VLMs) as judges of CUA trajectories. But a fundamental question has long gone unexamined: are these VLM judges reliable enough? To study it systematically, we introduce OSReward, a realistic, high-quality benchmark that evaluates VLM judges on CUA trajectories. The trajectories come from diverse agent backbones executing human-verified instructions across platforms, then rigorously labeled with ground-truth verdicts through multi-stage human annotation. Building on it, we derive OSReward-Hard, a challenge set concentrating genuinely hard cases, and OSReward-Multi for fine-grained efficiency and alignment scoring. The most comprehensive evaluation of VLM judges to date finds even state-of-the-art models fall short of an ideal judge, sharing a systematic leniency bias that mislabels failed runs as successes. The few reliable enough to trust are too expensive to run at scale, while affordable open models trail far behind. To close this gap, we construct and release OS-Shepherd-100K, an open corpus of reasoning-annotated trajectory judgments for the CUA community. On it, we train OS-Shepherd (9B and 35B), open reward models that supply low-cost, stable, and reliable reward signals, matching commercial judges at 30-60% lower cost than the frontier. Extensive analyses further inform the design of reliable CUA reward at scale. Our code, benchmark, dataset, and model checkpoints are available at https://os-copilot.github.io/OSReward-Home/.

深度讲解Deep dive

OSReward 用跨 Web、Windows、Ubuntu 和 Android 的人工金标轨迹测试 computer-use judge，发现主流 VLM 普遍过度接受未完成任务；OS-Shepherd 再以一致性筛选、SFT 和定向 GRPO改善失败召回。

它解决什么问题Problem

CUA 评测、数据筛选和 RL 都要判断整条 GUI 轨迹是否完成任务，但人工 verifier 难以扩展，现有 VLM judge 的跨平台可靠性和系统偏差此前缺乏人工金标测量。

核心方法Core method

作者在四个平台构造真实应用状态和任务，由多种 agent 执行，过滤环境事故后让三名标注者读取完整截图、思考和动作，再由高级 reviewer 解决分歧，得到 1019 条金标轨迹及 284 条 Hard 子集。统一 judge 协议输入完整文本历史和默认最后五帧；OS-Shepherd-100K 则从 321631 个自动 judge instance 中按跨 judge/截图配置高一致性筛选，先 SFT，再从可恢复 false-success 中挖约 3100 条样本做短程 GRPO。

怎么工作How it works

  1. 构造真实跨平台环境和经审核任务。
  2. 运行多种 agent 并过滤网络阻断、应用崩溃等环境故障。
  3. 三人标注并经 meta-review 形成 Full、Hard 和 Multi 数据。
  4. 按固定输入与解码协议评测 VLM judge 并分析宽松偏差。
  5. 用一致性语料执行 SFT，再以 false-success 定向 GRPO 训练 OS-Shepherd。

论文报告了什么Reported findings

  • 论文表 1 报告：Full 上最佳 judge 准确率/平衡准确率为 89.7%/90.0%，Hard 上降为 69.7%/69.7%；Hard 的平均准确率约 52%。
  • 论文第 4.3 节报告：过度接受未完成任务约占全部错误的三分之二，对每个 judge 至少占其错误的 48%。
  • 论文第 5 节报告：删去每步思考与动作文本平均损失 7.2 个百分点，并翻转 22.7% 的 verdict。
  • 论文表 4 报告：OS-Shepherd-9B 的 Full/Hard 为 86.1%/60.2%，35B-A3B 为 85.6%/62.7%。
对 StreamingModelHarness 的帮助（编辑判断）Fit for StreamingModelHarness (editorial analysis)
  • 编辑判断：它适合作为 StreamingModelHarness 的离线评测和训练奖励层，在 endpoint/commit 后等一轮工具轨迹完成，再用完整文本历史和尾部画面判断结果。
  • 编辑判断：晚注入工具可以写入轨迹后统一评分，但 OS-Shepherd 不负责实时工具编排；它也不提供边听边说、模型层流式或全双工能力。

边界与局限Limits

  • Full 只有 1019 条、覆盖四个平台且没有 macOS；Hard 按人工分歧和失败富集，不代表真实部署分布。
  • 主协议只看最后五帧；论文外部分析中，轨迹超过 16 步时准确率由 0.76 降至 0.57。
  • OS-Shepherd-100K 是 judge ensemble 的高一致性自动标签，不是人工金标，仍可能保留共享偏差。
  • Multi 只覆盖成功轨迹，细粒度 alignment 样本有限，且与人工 verifier 的一致性不等于重新人工核验后的真实准确率。

代码与模型开放情况Code and model availability

部分开放Partial   9B/35B-A3B 权重已按 Apache-2.0 开放，Full/Hard 与 OS-Shepherd-100K 可获取；训练流水线尚未齐全，代码仓库和数据卡未声明许可证，训练语料还需人工批准。
