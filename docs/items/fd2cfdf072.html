Overcoming State Inertia in Full-Duplex Spoken Language Models via Activation Steering

论文Paper · arxiv · 2026-06-09 · ◈ 12
摘要Summary
该工作分析全双工口语模型隐层表示，发现听与说两条流具有各自特异的预测模式，并据此用激活引导（activation steering）克服模型在听说切换间的状态惯性。它为"边说边听"的协调性提供了可操作的机理洞见，提示我们不必重训模型也能在推理期改善全双工的听说切换行为。 Full-duplex spoken language models (FD-SLMs) enable seamless speech interaction by allowing models to listen and speak simultaneously, yet the internal mechanism by which they coordinate listening and speaking remains underexplored.
