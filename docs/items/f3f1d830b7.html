VAD: Attributing Visual Evidence for Target Reconstruction in Multimodal On-Policy Distillation

论文Paper · hf · 2026-07-29 · ◈ 10
摘要Summary
VAD 用同一教师在有、无视觉证据下的反事实差分重建蒸馏目标，六个基准平均分达到 78.32（4B）和 79.93（9B）。它可启发离线学习“哪些 token 真由工具回执支持”，但不是 StreamingModelHarness 的运行时流式、判停或 TTS 组件。 Multimodal on-policy distillation (OPD) transfers fine-grained visual knowledge by supervising student-generated trajectories with a privileged-view teacher. Yet its next-token corrections are source-mixed, combining visual signals with linguistic priors and teacher-specific effects. The key challenge is to estimate which corrections are supported by visual evidence, not merely where or how strongly to distill. We introduce Visual Attribution Distillation (VAD), a counterfactual target-reconstruction algorithm that estimates the visually attributable part of a teacher correction. At each student-generated prefix, VAD evaluates the same fixed teacher with the relevant evidence present and removed. The corresponding change in centered log-probabilities defines ut, a signed proxy for the visual evidence direction that estimates how revealing the evidence supports or refutes candidate tokens. VAD projects the original correction onto this proxy to obtain an intervention-aligned component and a proxy-unexplained residual, then reconstructs a student-anchored target from the former. During training, this reconstructed target supplies the primary supervision signal, while the privileged teacher contributes a weak regularizer. Across six fine-grained visual benchmarks at 4B and 9B scales, VAD outperforms direct privileged-view distillation and visual-advantage weighting. Token- level and controlled-target analyses show that the proxy-aligned component is enriched in task-relevant visual corrections and yields stronger target shifts, especially when evidence refutes a mistaken answer. These results support counterfactual target reconstruction as an effective alternative to source-mixed supervision.

深度讲解Deep dive

VAD 通过同一教师看有、无视觉证据的反事实差分，只蒸馏可归因于图像的 token 修正，减少语言先验和教师偏好的混入。

它解决什么问题Problem

多模态 on-policy distillation 的教师修正同时包含视觉证据、语言先验和教师特有偏好；仅按教师—学生分歧加权，无法判断修正是否真的由图像支持。

核心方法Core method

学生先基于完整图像生成轨迹；固定教师在同一前缀下分别观察证据裁剪和退化裁剪，以中心化 log-prob 差构造带符号视觉方向。VAD 将教师修正投影到该方向，围绕学生分布重建目标，并以 JSD 为主损失、弱教师匹配项为正则。该过程只用于训练，推理仍是单个完整图像学生。

怎么工作How it works

  1. 学生在完整图像上生成 on-policy response。
  2. 教师对同一前缀分别读取有证据和退化证据的视图。
  3. 用两次预测差估计视觉归因方向并分解教师修正。
  4. 围绕学生分布重建目标并训练；部署时移除教师和反事实分支。

论文报告了什么Reported findings

  • 六个基准平均分达到 78.32（4B）和 79.93（9B），分别领先同规模最佳替代方法 2.40 和 2.80 点。
  • 强教师修正中仅 23.2%（4B）和 22.8%（9B）同时具有强视觉对齐；视觉属性、对象和决策 token 在归因分量中的占比为 42.0%。
对 StreamingModelHarness 的帮助（编辑判断）Fit for StreamingModelHarness (editorial analysis)
  • 可把有、无工具回执视作离线反事实干预，研究只蒸馏真正由回执支持的 token，但这是超出论文视觉实验的编辑推演。
  • 它不提供流式音频、增量 KV、判停、晚注入接口或 TTS 优化，应视为未来训练目标而非运行时组件。

边界与局限Limits

  • 单一视图对只提供一个对比方向，归因与残差都不是可识别的因果分离。
  • 实验限于静态细粒度视觉问答，并依赖大模型 judge，未验证语音、工具或流式场景。
