MMOOC: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Out-of-Context Evaluation in Multimodal Large Language Models

论文Paper · hf · 2026-07-31 · ◈ 7
摘要Summary
MMOOC 用 41K+ 图问对同时评测 MLLM 在真正 OOC 时拒答、在 Shifted IC 时仍回答的能力，并显示当前模型难以二者兼顾。可作座舱多模态拒答回归参考，但不涉及 StreamingModelHarness 的流式判停或语音双工。 Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) have achieved strong performance on a wide range of vision-language tasks, but often fail under imperfect or shifted contexts. A reliable MLLM should refuse truly out-of-context (OOC) questions with subject-level context shifts while still answering shifted in-context (Shifted IC) questions with non-subject context shifts. Existing benchmarks mainly target OOC or visually unanswerable questions, but overlook answerable Shifted IC cases and cover limited OOC shifts. To fill this gap, we present MMOOC, a large-scale benchmark for evaluating refusal and robust answering abilities of MLLMs. MMOOC contains over 41K image-question pairs, including answerable Shifted IC cases and unanswerable OOC cases, spanning three question formats, eight shift types and six visual scenarios, with data quality ensured through MLLM-based filtering and human verification. We evaluate model responses using Accuracy and Refusal Rate, and further introduce an LLM-as-a-Judge metric to assess the correctness of model reasoning. Experiments on diverse MLLMs show that current models still struggle to balance answer-ability and refusal under shifted contexts. We further analyze key failure patterns and show that post-training can improve robustness. MMOOC will be made publicly available.

深度讲解Deep dive

MMOOC 是面向 MLLM 的大规模上下文偏移基准：同时测真正 OOC 时应拒绝，以及 Shifted IC 时仍应回答。

它解决什么问题Problem

MLLM 在不完美/偏移视觉上下文下不可靠；现有基准偏重 OOC 或不可答问题，忽视仍可答的 Shifted IC，且 OOC 偏移类型覆盖有限。

核心方法Core method

构建含 41K+ 图问对的基准，覆盖三种题型、八类偏移、六类视觉场景；用 Accuracy/Refusal Rate，并以 LLM-as-a-Judge 评估推理合理性；系统评测 18 个开源与闭源 MLLM，并分析后训练改善。

怎么工作How it works

  1. 按 Shifted IC 与 OOC 分类生成/筛选图问对。
  2. MLLM 过滤 + 人工核验控制质量。
  3. 对 13 个开源与 5 个闭源模型评回答与拒绝。
  4. 用 Judge 指标分析失败模式与后训练效果。

论文报告了什么Reported findings

  • 基准规模超过 41K 图问对，并评测 18 个代表性 MLLM。
  • 实验表明当前模型仍难在偏移上下文下平衡“可答性保持”与“恰当拒绝”；更强通用能力或更大模型规模并不必然带来更好 OOC 稳健性。
  • 论文报告 Judge 与人类在 Refusal Rate 上一致率为 96.78%，Rationality 为 89.34%。
对 StreamingModelHarness 的帮助（编辑判断）Fit for StreamingModelHarness (editorial analysis)
  • 对座舱视觉问答的“拒答/幻觉”治理有评测价值，可作回归集思路参考。
  • 与全双工语音 harness 无直接机制对应；不要当成流式双工方案。
  • 若多模态座舱助手会遇到错误前提问题，可借鉴其 Shifted IC vs OOC 区分。

边界与局限Limits

  • 静态图问基准，不覆盖连续视频/语音流与实时打断。
  • 官方 GitHub 仍显示数据集与评测工具“Coming Soon”，复现材料不完整。
