Better Adherence, Richer Context: A Field Evaluation of LLM-Powered Conversational Voice Diaries for Sleep

论文Paper · arxiv · 2026-06-17 · ◈ 7
摘要Summary
四周实地研究中，LLM 对话式音箱睡眠日记完成率约 92%，高于文本表单约 71%，并产生显著更长的情境叙述。其主动追问可借鉴到 StreamingModelHarness 多轮补槽，但不是流式全双工方案。 Sleep diaries are central to behavioral sleep medicine and cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia, yet daily completion is difficult to sustain, and static forms often provide limited context for interpreting night-to-night sleep variation. We designed an LLM-powered conversational voice diary that delivers clinically grounded morning and evening sleep diary questions through proactive smart-speaker prompts, structured conversational intake, and adaptive follow-up dialogue. We evaluated the system in a four-week between-subjects field study with 30 university students, comparing it with a text-based mobile diary using matched diary items, reporting windows, and reminder intervals. Compared with the text-based diary, the conversational voice diary showed higher adherence and elicited more detailed contextual self-report about routines, stressors, environmental conditions, and other sleep-related factors. Participants also described the voice diary as easier to integrate into daily routines, despite longer perceived completion time. However, voice-based conversational intake produced lower completeness for some structured diary fields, revealing a trade-off between expressive richness and structured precision. These findings show both the promise and the challenge of using LLM-powered conversational voice assistants for longitudinal health self-report.

深度讲解Deep dive

四周实地对比显示，LLM 驱动的智能音箱睡眠日记比文本表单依从性更高（完成率约 92% vs 71%），并引出更丰富的情境自述。

它解决什么问题Problem

睡眠日记对行为睡眠医学很关键，但每日填表难坚持，静态表单也缺少解释夜间变化的情境信息。

核心方法Core method

设计由 LLM 驱动的对话式语音日记：在晨间/晚间窗口用智能音箱主动提醒，按临床睡眠日记结构提问并做自适应追问。30 名大学生、四周、组间对照（15 语音助手 vs 15 文本表单）。

怎么工作How it works

  1. 按晨/晚窗口主动提示用户开始日记。
  2. 结构化采集睡眠日记核心项，并用 LLM 追问情境细节。
  3. 记录完整对话并与文本表单条目对齐比较。
  4. 统计依从性、可用性、感知耗时与信息密度等指标。

论文报告了什么Reported findings

  • 语音条件完成率 M=92.00（SD=7.82）显著高于文本 M=70.79（SD=28.54），t(16.09)=2.78，p=.007。
  • SUS 可用性：语音 M=77.00 高于文本 M=68.75（单尾 Mann–Whitney，p=.042）。
  • 条目更长：token 数语音 M=99.26 vs 文本 23.95（p<.001）；但感知完成时间语音更长（约 6.06 vs 3.13 分钟）。
对 StreamingModelHarness 的帮助（编辑判断）Fit for StreamingModelHarness (editorial analysis)
  • 编辑判断：主动提醒+结构化追问可借鉴到座舱多轮槽位补全，减少一次说不全的车控/日程意图。
  • 编辑判断：系统是回合式音箱对话，不是 Harness 的输入流式+判停后生成；也不证明原生全双工。
  • 编辑判断：更长口播与更高感知耗时提醒：座舱追问要设轮次/时延预算，避免打扰驾驶。

边界与局限Limits

  • 样本为 30 名大学生、四周，外推临床失眠人群与长期依从性有限。
  • 分组按取设备顺序而非完全随机。
  • 实现依赖商用智能音箱与 LLM 栈，论文未提供可复现开源系统。
