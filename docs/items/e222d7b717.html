AffectDF: The Most Comprehensive Benchmark for Speech Deepfake Detection against Emotionally Expressive Attacks

论文Paper · arxiv · 2026-08-06 · ◈ 5
摘要Summary
AffectDF 是面向情绪化语音深度伪造检测的大规模基准，覆盖约260小时、21种攻击与五种情绪。对 StreamingModelHarness 的启示是：外挂TTS/合成语音链路需要额外的情绪与韵律鲁棒防伪门，不能只依赖中性语料上的反欺诈模型。 Speech deepfake detection (SDD) systems achieve strong performance on conventional benchmarks; however, existing datasets provide limited coverage of emotionally expressive and recent large audio-language model (LALM)-based attacks. Existing emotional spoofing datasets are also limited in scale and attack diversity, typically covering only voice conversion (VC) or text-to-speech (TTS) attacks. We introduce AffectDF, the most comprehensive benchmark for emotionally expressive speech deepfakes, spanning TTS, VC, emotional VC, and LALM-based spoofing attacks across both acted and spontaneous emotional speech. AffectDF contains approximately 260 hours of speech generated using 21 spoofing attacks across five emotional states. We benchmark state-of-the-art SDD systems under conventional and emotional spoofing conditions, including LALM-based detectors evaluated with both inference-only prompting and supervised fine-tuning. Our experiments reveal severe robustness degradation when models trained on conventional benchmarks are evaluated on AffectDF, with several systems approaching near-random performance. Surprisingly, even large-scale emotional training does not consistently improve cross-domain robustness, indicating that current SDD systems fail to learn generalized spoof representations under emotional and prosodic variability. Robustness further varies substantially across emotional states, attack families, and acted vs spontaneous emotional speech conditions. These findings expose fundamental limitations of current SDD systems and establish AffectDF as a benchmark for developing more robust spoof detection models.

深度讲解Deep dive

AffectDF 是覆盖约260小时、21种攻击与五种情绪的语音深度伪造基准，显示常规反欺诈模型在情绪化与 LALM 攻击下会严重退化。

它解决什么问题Problem

现有语音深度伪造检测基准对情绪表达与大音频语言模型（LALM）攻击覆盖不足；情绪伪造数据也多局限于少量 VC 或 TTS 攻击，难衡量真实富情绪伪造场景。

核心方法Core method

作者构建 AffectDF，跨 TTS、VC、情绪 VC 与 LALM 伪造，覆盖 acted/spontaneous 情绪语音与中性、高兴、愤怒、悲伤、惊讶五态。随后在常规与情绪伪造设定下评测 RawNet2、AASIST、XLSR-SLS、XLSR-Mamba、ProSDD 等，并包含仅推理提示与监督微调的 LALM 检测器。

怎么工作How it works

  1. 用多种合成/转换与 LALM 情绪语音生成攻击样本，形成约260小时、21类攻击数据。
  2. 按情绪、攻击族与 acted/spontaneous 条件组织评测协议。
  3. 以 EER 为主指标，比较在 ASVspoof 等常规训练集上训练的检测器迁移到 AffectDF 的表现。
  4. 进一步在 AffectDF 上训练/微调，观察跨情绪与跨攻击族鲁棒性变化。

论文报告了什么Reported findings

  • AffectDF 约含260小时语音、21种伪造攻击与五种情绪状态。
  • 在 AffectDF 上，RawNet2 与 AASIST 的 EER 分别升至59.71%与56.40%，接近随机。
  • 在所评系统中，ProSDD 在 AffectDF 上表现最强，EER 为12.49%。
  • 即便大规模情绪训练，也不总能稳定提升跨域鲁棒性；不同情绪、攻击族与 acted/spontaneous 条件下差距显著。
对 StreamingModelHarness 的帮助（编辑判断）Fit for StreamingModelHarness (editorial analysis)
  • 可作为座舱外挂 TTS/语音克隆防伪门的压力测试集，尤其覆盖情绪化播报与指令仿冒。
  • 不应假设中性 ASVspoof 训练的检测器可直接挂到 StreamingModelHarness；需按情绪与合成器族单独校准阈值。
  • Harness 仍是输入流式、判停后生成、晚注入工具与外挂 TTS，防伪应放在音频准入旁路，而不是改成模型内原生双工。

边界与局限Limits

  • 主贡献是基准与评测，不提供可直接部署的端到端座舱防伪服务。
  • 攻击来自公开合成模型与数据集协议，真实车内噪声、回声与多说话人场景仍需另测。
  • 数据按 CC BY-NC 4.0 研究非商用发布，商用复用需另行核验。
