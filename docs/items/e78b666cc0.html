TeleWorld: Towards Dynamic Multimodal Synthesis with a 4D World Model

论文Paper · arxiv · 2025-12-31 · ◈ 9
摘要Summary
TeleWorld 将视频生成、实时4D重建与长时世界记忆组成生成-重建-引导闭环，并报告约8 FPS的高分辨率实时生成。对座舱多模态世界记忆有概念参考，但远超当前 StreamingModelHarness 的语音主链路范围。 World models aim to endow AI systems with the ability to represent, generate, and interact with dynamic environments in a coherent and temporally consistent manner. While recent video generation models have demonstrated impressive visual quality, they remain limited in real-time interaction, long-horizon consistency, and persistent memory of dynamic scenes, hindering their evolution into practical world models. In this report, we present TeleWorld, a real-time multimodal 4D world modeling framework that unifies video generation, dynamic scene reconstruction, and long-term world memory within a closed-loop system. TeleWorld introduces a novel generation-reconstruction-guidance paradigm, where generated video streams are continuously reconstructed into a dynamic 4D spatio-temporal representation, which in turn guides subsequent generation to maintain spatial, temporal, and physical consistency. To support long-horizon generation with low latency, we employ an autoregressive diffusion-based video model enhanced with Macro-from-Micro Planning (MMPL)--a hierarchical planning method that reduces error accumulation from frame-level to segment-level-alongside efficient Distribution Matching Distillation (DMD), enabling real-time synthesis under practical computational budgets. Our approach achieves seamless integration of dynamic object modeling and static scene representation within a unified 4D framework, advancing world models toward practical, interactive, and computationally accessible systems. Extensive experiments demonstrate that TeleWorld achieves strong performance in both static and dynamic world understanding, long-term consistency, and real-time generation efficiency, positioning it as a practical step toward interactive, memory-enabled world models for multimodal generation and embodied intelligence.

深度讲解Deep dive

TeleWorld 以生成-重建-引导闭环做实时多模态4D世界模型，结合 MMPL 与 DMD，并报告 TeleWorld-18B 约8 FPS 的高分辨率生成。

它解决什么问题Problem

视频生成模型画质强，但在实时交互、长程一致性与动态场景持久记忆上不足，难成为可互动的世界模型。

核心方法Core method

提出 TeleWorld：自回归扩散视频生成、实时4D重建与长期记忆闭环；用 Macro-from-Micro Planning 降低误差累积，并用 Distribution Matching Distillation 与系统优化支持实时合成。

怎么工作How it works

  1. 根据用户指令自回归生成视频片段。
  2. 将生成流实时重建为动态4D表征/点云记忆。
  3. 用重建结果回导后续生成以维持时空一致。
  4. 通过分层规划与蒸馏维持长程低延迟吞吐。

论文报告了什么Reported findings

  • 文称 TeleWorld-1.3B 长程生成吞吐可超32 FPS；TeleWorld-18B 在四卡设定下对960×1760视频稳定约8 FPS。
  • 超分解码约17 FPS（960×1760）。
  • 在 WorldScores 等相关评测中报告领先，静态世界生成比最强基线高0.61分，动态相关项高7.61分（按正文表述）。
  • 训练系统可把 TeleWorld-18B 的 DMD 训练压到32×H100 量级。
对 StreamingModelHarness 的帮助（编辑判断）Fit for StreamingModelHarness (editorial analysis)
  • 对生成后回写世界状态再指导下一步的闭环记忆有概念价值。
  • 远超当前 StreamingModelHarness 的语音主业；不宜把它写成可直接替换判停/TTS 管线的方案。
  • 若未来座舱需要场景级多模态记忆，可参考其重建-引导接口，而不是端到端搬用18B视频栈。

边界与局限Limits

  • 算力与系统复杂度高，边缘座舱难直接部署。
  • 论文引用 TeleTron 训练基础设施，但不等于 TeleWorld 完整权重/应用代码已开源。
  • 评测偏世界生成与一致性，不含语音全双工交互指标。
