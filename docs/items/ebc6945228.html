LEDGERMIND: Provenance-Constrained Multimodal Agentic Reasoning with a Structured Evidence Ledger

论文Paper · hf · 2026-07-29 · ◈ 13
摘要Summary
LedgerMind 将多模态代理的自由文本轨迹改造成有来源约束的结构化证据账本，并检查引用、实体与数字是否真正得到工具结果支持。它在多项静态图文推理基准上报告明显增益，但实现、Hard-200 和审计数据均未公开，部分模型版本和对照标注也不一致，因此更适合作为可审计工具运行时的设计参考。 Multimodal agents for visual question answering increasingly operate as multi-step trajectories that interleave perception, retrieval, and reasoning, yet evaluation still largely reduces to final-answer accuracy. This aggregate signal cannot tell whether a correct answer was reached through grounded evidence, language priors, or accidental error cancellation. We propose to treat a multimodal agent trajectory as a provenance-constrained state machine: tool outputs are normalized into a Structured Evidence Ledger that serves as the trajectory state, downstream reasoning and decision claims may cite only active ledger entries, grounding is checked at the entity and numeric level, and repair is realized as typed state transitions that cannot introduce content without tool-produced provenance. We instantiate this design as LedgerMind (Provenance-Constrained Multimodal Agentic Reasoning with a Structured Evidence Ledger), augmented by a Three-Layer Grounding Protocol, an Adaptive Dual-Path Dispatcher that matches reasoning depth to question complexity, and an Event-Triggered Verification-and-Repair engine with a formal provenance non-amplification guarantee. We use LedgerMind to target four recurring failure patterns that final-answer accuracy tends to obscure: unsupported intermediate reasoning, citation-backed entity hallucination (Phantom Grounding), over-reasoning on simple queries, and repair-time amplification. Experiments across multiple multimodal reasoning benchmarks and backbone MLLMs show that LedgerMind improves both answer accuracy and trajectory-level faithfulness.

深度讲解Deep dive

LedgerMind 是套在冻结多模态模型外的证据运行时：工具输出先写入结构化账本，后续声明必须引用有效证据，并接受实体和数字一致性检查。

它解决什么问题Problem

只看最终答案无法区分真实取证、语言先验碰巧命中和错误相消；自由文本轨迹还会产生无依据中间声明、伪落地和在修复阶段继续扩大的幻觉。

核心方法Core method

方法使用 11 字段 Structured Evidence Ledger、支持覆盖率、实体一致性 ECC、数字一致性 NCC、Direct/FullPipe 分发器和七种有限类型的修复操作。修复新增的账本内容必须来自工具输出，但这一来源约束不保证工具本身正确。

怎么工作How it works

  1. 规则分发器选择直接回答或完整取证流程。
  2. 把 OCR、裁剪和搜索结果规范化为感知、检索或推导账本项。
  3. 状态声明按 Evidence、Inference、Judgment 分层生成并引用 active 条目。
  4. 依次检查覆盖率、ECC 和 NCC，失败时触发 Drop、Refresh、Retry、Acquire 或 Abstain 等局部修复。
  5. 返回答案以及可审计的证据和修复轨迹。

论文报告了什么Reported findings

  • VTC-Bench 上 LedgerMind(Gemini-3-Flash) 报告 58.90%；Hard-200 六个 backbone 的 Overall 增益为 11.25 至 19.75 个百分点。
  • MMMU-Pro 消融中完整系统为 68.89，去掉账本降至 53.50，去掉 typed repair 降至 60.40，去掉 ECC/NCC 降至 63.70。
  • EMMA 最佳配置 Overall 为 58.29，相对对应 baseline 增加 9.58 个百分点；Coding 子项反而下降 0.18 个百分点。
对 StreamingModelHarness 的帮助（编辑判断）Fit for StreamingModelHarness (editorial analysis)
  • 编辑判断：最适合用于 Harness 晚注入工具的证据生命周期、依赖关系、数值声明和修复审计，把自由文本反思收敛为有限状态转换。
  • 编辑判断：它只覆盖 commit 后的静态图文推理，没有视频流、增量解码、语音打断或 TTS，不能改变 Harness 非模型原生全双工的事实。

边界与局限Limits

  • 论文只研究静态图文 VQA；ECC/NCC 依赖确定性抽取和小型别名表，来源可追溯也不等于事实正确。
  • Hard-200 含 25 条未公开的 RealCAR，固定外部审计器也会引入模型偏差。
  • 论文未统一报告账本、重复读数、搜索和修复的延迟、token 或 API 成本，部分模型数量和 VTC 对照标注不一致。
