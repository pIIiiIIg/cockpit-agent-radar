SmartMage: Dynamic Modality Orchestration for 3D Scene Understanding

论文Paper · hf · 2026-08-04 · ◈ 4
摘要Summary
SmartMage 把 3D 推理拆成问题驱动的模态选择和模态感知专家路由，优于固定全模态融合。它可启发 StreamingModelHarness 在 commit 后选择输入或工具，但需要模型内部训练，且当前仓库仍是占位状态，不涉及流式语音或全双工。 Understanding 3D scenes is fundamental to embodied intelligence, requiring joint reasoning over heterogeneous information from multiple modalities, including visual and geometric cues. However, the relevance of these modalities often varies across queries. Existing Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) typically rely on fixed modality combinations, overlooking query-dependent modality needs. Such a rigid design can introduce semantic noise from irrelevant modalities while underutilizing more informative ones, leading to wasted computation and diluted reasoning. To address these challenges, this paper proposes SmartMage, a unified MLLM that dynamically orchestrates heterogeneous modalities for semantic-aware 3D scene understanding. Specifically, SmartMage incorporates: (1) a Semantic-guided Modality Adaptive RouTng (SMART) module that selects task-relevant modalities using semantic priors, text-modality alignment, and modality quality; and (2) a Modality-Aware Gating Expert (MAGE) module that leverages modality priors to guide expert activation, fostering adaptive specialization in multimodal reasoning. Empirically, SmartMage achieves state-of-the-art performance across five 3D scene understanding benchmarks, and attains competitive results on RGB-only video understanding benchmarks. In our diagnostic benchmark ScanFacet, tasks are divided into fine-grained semantic categories, enabling analysis of modality combinations preferred by each semantic type. The observed modality-semantic patterns provide further evidence of SmartMage's effectiveness. Project page: https://yuecheong.github.io/SmartMage/.

深度讲解Deep dive

SmartMage 先按问题选择 RGB、深度、BEV、点云和体素，再让稀疏 MoE 专家按模态分工，以减少固定全模态融合的冗余和干扰。

它解决什么问题Problem

3D MLLM 常固定拼接所有模态，但颜色、计数、位置和形状问题依赖的证据不同；无差别加入模态会带来语义噪声、计算浪费和推理信号稀释。

核心方法Core method

RGB、深度和 BEV 经 Qwen3-VL 视觉塔，点云经 PointNet++，体素经 Mask3D sparse U-Net，再统一投影到 4096 维。SMART 路由综合问题语义先验、文本—模态相似度、质量估计和 RGB evidence gate，推理时按累计概率 0.8 选最多三个辅助模态；MAGE 在 LLM 多层插入 8 专家、top-2 MoE，以 token 模态预测和模态—专家亲和约束促进专家分工。

怎么工作How it works

  1. 从 RGB-D 视频、mesh 和相机姿态构造五种模态。
  2. 用 FoVSR 选出 32 个覆盖率高且清晰的关键帧。
  3. 编码并对齐各模态 token。
  4. SMART 根据问题选择辅助模态。
  5. MAGE 将 token 路由到模态偏好的专家并生成答案、定位或描述。

论文报告了什么Reported findings

  • 论文表 1 报告：ScanQA/SQA3D EM@1 为 32.6/66.8，ScanRefer Acc@0.25/0.5 为 65.9/59.5，Multi3DRefer F1@0.25/0.5 为 65.4/60.7。
  • 论文表 4 报告：动态路由五项主指标为 32.6/66.8/88.7/59.5/60.7，固定融合全部模态为 30.7/64.5/83.8/57.8/57.2。
  • 论文表 5 报告：训练为 47.44 秒/iter，TTFT 112.5ms、E2E 538.2ms；另需 48.5ms 的 2D 和 64.8ms 的 3D 预处理。
对 StreamingModelHarness 的帮助（编辑判断）Fit for StreamingModelHarness (editorial analysis)
  • 编辑判断：问题驱动的动态模态选择可启发 StreamingModelHarness 在 endpoint/commit 后选择输入模态或工具。
  • 编辑判断：SmartMage 需要训练模型内部路由和 MoE，且 3D 特征基本预先构造；它不处理流式输入、生成中晚注入工具、外置 TTS 或全双工。

边界与局限Limits

  • 实验集中在 ScanNet 派生的室内数据，没有验证室外、移动传感器或跨数据集泛化。
  • 系统依赖 mesh、相机姿态、语义标签及 Mask3D proposals，部署前提较强。
  • 正文与附录对 MQE descriptor 的描述不同，存在复现歧义；ScanFacet 材质类也只有 55 个样本。
  • 官方仓库仍标注训练和评测代码 coming soon，专用权重与 ScanFacet 发布物也不可用。
