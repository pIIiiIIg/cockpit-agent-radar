Evo-Bench: Can Language Models Improve Agent Harness?

论文Paper · hf · 2026-08-09 · ◈ 8
摘要Summary
Evo-Bench 专门评测 LLM 能否长期改进自己的 agent harness，顶尖模型相对 CodeAct 可提升约 16.6 分。对 StreamingModelHarness，它提供自动化改框架的评测范式，但任务域并非语音全双工，不能直接替代座舱实时链路回归。 Large Language Models (LLMs) have driven rapid progress in autonomous agents, yet standard evaluations remain confined to static task solving. An emerging frontier is harness evolution---the agent's capacity to autonomously optimize its own operating harness. However, systematically benchmarking this capability remains challenging, as existing evaluations fail to isolate harness improvements from base model strength, prevent task-specific overfitting, or capture long-horizon iterative research. To address these challenges, we introduce Evo-Bench, the first benchmark designed to evaluate models' intrinsic harness-evolving capabilities across Search, Office, and General agent domains. To rigorously isolate this capability, Evo-Bench employs a novel harness-guided construction framework: it leverages auxiliary-task evolution to identify tasks genuinely sensitive to framework improvements, followed by sensitivity-aware stratified splitting to ensure robust cross-suite generalization. Extensive evaluations across nine frontier and open-weight models reveal that top models achieve massive absolute gains reaching 16.6 points, closely approaching state-of-the-art human-engineered baselines. Crucially, while autonomous evolution outpeforms artificial harness in General tasks and excels in Search tasks, it struggles in Office tasks that demand highly specific processing workflows. Furthermore, our analysis exposes critical temporal anomalies like early saturation, while demonstrating that the synthesized harnesses act as highly transferable reasoning structures, consistently boosting diverse policy models.

深度讲解Deep dive

Evo-Bench 评测模型能否在 Search/Office/General 域长期自改 agent harness；顶尖模型相对 CodeAct 基线最高提升 16.6 分。

它解决什么问题Problem

标准 agent 评测只看静态解题，无法分离 harness 改进与基座模型强度，也难防过拟合与检验长程迭代研发能力。

核心方法Core method

用辅助任务演化筛出对框架改进敏感的题目，再做敏感性分层切分以保证跨套泛化。模型在受控长程设定中诊断失败、改 harness 代码并迭代；在 Search、Office、General 三域对比九个前沿/开源权重模型与人工工程 harness。

怎么工作How it works

  1. 基于 harness 敏感性构造并分层划分验证/评测任务。
  2. 从种子 harness 出发多轮改代码并在验证集反馈。
  3. 在三域评测集上打分，报告相对 CodeAct 的绝对增益。
  4. 分析早饱和与合成 harness 对其他策略模型的可迁移性。

论文报告了什么Reported findings

  • 九个模型中 GPT-5.6 Sol Overall 46.3（相对 CodeAct +16.6）；Claude Opus 4.8 为 45.8（+16.1）。
  • 自主演化在 General 可超过人工 harness，在 Search 增益大，但 Office 因流程高度特定而挣扎。
  • 分析发现早期饱和等时间异常；合成 harness 可作为可迁移推理结构提升不同策略模型。
  • 排行榜同时报告 AnytimeVal 等过程指标。
对 StreamingModelHarness 的帮助（编辑判断）Fit for StreamingModelHarness (editorial analysis)
  • 编辑判断：直接对标“能否改 harness”，可为 StreamingModelHarness 的自动化重构与回归提供评测模板。
  • 编辑判断：当前 Harness 的流式输入、判停、晚工具、外挂 TTS 属于语音实时边界，Evo-Bench 任务域并不覆盖这些；迁移时需自建语音敏感任务。
  • 编辑判断：不要把模型自改 harness 的分数误读为已具备车载安全认证；高风险变更仍需人工门控。

边界与局限Limits

  • Office 域对高度特定工作流仍然困难，说明演化能力不均衡。
  • 依赖可执行沙箱与外部环境，复现成本高。
  • 早饱和意味着更长预算未必线性换来更好 harness。
  • 排行榜含闭源模型，完整公平复现受限。
