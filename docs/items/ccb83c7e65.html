Normal-Anchored First-Order Model-Agnostic Meta-Learning based Whisper Fine-Tuning for Enhancing Fairness of Cleft Lip and Palate Speech Recognition

论文Paper · arxiv · 2026-07-31 · ◈ 5
摘要Summary
NA-FOMAML 用正常语音作内循环锚点、不同腭裂严重度作外循环任务微调 Whisper，使两个数据集的 CLP Macro WER 降至 24.58% 和 30.40%，但重度 WER 仍超过 52%。仅在可访问性目标明确时适合 Harness A/C 的并行 ASR 支路，不能据此声称解决流式判停或公平性。 Automatic speech recognition (ASR) for cleft lip and palate (CLP) speech is difficult because acoustic and articulatory patterns vary across severity levels. This variability reduces the performance of pretrained ASR systems, and conventional fine-tuning may not generalize well under low-resource, heterogeneous CLP conditions. This work proposes Normal-Anchored First-Order Model-Agnostic Meta-Learning (NA-FOMAML) for adapting Whisper to CLP speech. The method uses a first-order bilevel meta-learning framework in which normal speech is used in the inner loop as a stable support condition, while CLP severity groups are used in the outer loop to improve post-adaptation robustness. This design aims to reduce the performance gap between normal and pathological speech. Experiments are conducted on the NMCPC and AIISH datasets using four normal-anchored training configurations. Frozen encoder, full encoder, and selected Whisper encoder-layer tuning strategies are evaluated, including layers 0--5, 4--11, 6--11, and 8--11, with decoder and projection-head adaptation. Results show that outer-loop training with only normal speech is insufficient. For NMCPC, full encoder tuning with Normal to Normal+Mild+Moderate gives WERs of 4.40%, 5.53%, 16.14%, and 52.07% for normal, mild, moderate, and severe speech. For AIISH, full encoder tuning with Normal to Normal+Mild+Moderate+Severe gives WERs of 2.48%, 19.66%, 14.05%, and 57.50%. A transcription-based phoneme-category analysis shows that severe CLP speech has high error rates across fricatives, affricates, nasals, liquids, plosives, and vowels. Overall, NA-FOMAML improves cross-severity robustness, but severe speech still requires severity-aware sampling, phoneme-aware loss functions, and augmentation targeting pressure consonant and resonance-related distortions.

深度讲解Deep dive

NA-FOMAML 用正常语音作内循环锚点、不同腭裂严重度作外循环任务来微调 Whisper，但重度语音 WER 仍超过 52%。

它解决什么问题Problem

腭裂语音样本少且严重度差异大，普通微调容易偏向清晰或多数语音，导致正常、轻度、中度和重度说话者之间存在显著识别差距。

核心方法Core method

方法以 Whisper-small 为基座，内循环 support 始终使用正常语音做一次临时梯度更新，外循环 query 比较从仅正常到包含全部严重度的四种组合，并采用不反传二阶导数的一阶 MAML。论文比较冻结、全量和不同 encoder 层段的调优方式，每 fold 训练 300 episodes，并以五折模型集成评估未见说话者。

怎么工作How it works

  1. 从正常语音 support batch 计算一次内循环更新。
  2. 用临时参数在指定严重度组合的 query batch 上计算损失。
  3. 以一阶 query 梯度更新原始 Whisper 参数。
  4. 在未见说话者上分别统计各严重度 WER 和音素类别错误。

论文报告了什么Reported findings

  • NMCPC 最佳配置的正常/轻度/中度/重度 WER 为 4.40/5.53/16.14/52.07%，CLP Macro 为 24.58%。
  • AIISH 最佳配置对应 2.48/19.66/14.05/57.50%，CLP Macro 为 30.40%；相对仅正常外循环的 75.11% 明显下降。
对 StreamingModelHarness 的帮助（编辑判断）Fit for StreamingModelHarness (editorial analysis)
  • 只有在病理语音可访问性目标明确时，才值得在 Harness A/C 的并行 ASR 支路试验，并用真实座舱 CLP 语音复测工具成功率。
  • 论文按整段 utterance 评测，不支持 partial transcript、endpointing 或边说边更新，也不影响晚工具注入和外挂 TTS。

边界与局限Limits

  • 缺少数据组成相同的普通微调对照，无法完全区分收益来自 FOMAML 还是加入更多病理语音。
  • 临床数据小且不公开，重度 WER 仍很高；超参数和预训练基线表格还存在不一致，削弱可复现性。
